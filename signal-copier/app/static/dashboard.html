Positions

"Exit now" submits an immediate market close for that one position. "Flatten" exits every open position on that account, one at a time. Both bypass routing rules and go straight to that specific account's broker.

Routing rules

Every account listed in "destinations" receives every signal matching "source" (and "symbol filter" if set) — this is fan-out, not disambiguation. Comma-separate multiple destinations/symbols.

Providers & analysts

Overrides an account's own multiplier/fixed_quantity/managed_lifecycle/enabled for signals from a given source (provider) or, more specifically, a given analyst within that source. Unset fields inherit from the next broader level. See README.md's "Providers and analysts" section.

Signal provider value & subscriptions

Realized P&L, win rate and profit factor per provider (and, within it, per asset class), replayed from this service's own confirmed executions and attributed by FIFO entry lot -- see GET /providers/value. A provider's subscription cost (below) is weighed against its realized P&L for the "still worth paying for" verdict. Not counted here: a managed-lifecycle position's stop-loss/target/trailing exit never creates the order row this replay reads -- a provider whose losses are mostly caught by a stop, rather than an explicit close signal, will look better here than it really is. Corroborate against "Managed-lifecycle coverage" above before cancelling or promoting anything on this alone.
