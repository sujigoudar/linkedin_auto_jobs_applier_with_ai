Positions

"Exit now" submits an immediate market close for that one position. "Flatten" exits every open position on that account, one at a time. Both bypass routing rules and go straight to that specific account's broker.

Routing rules

Every account listed in "destinations" receives every signal matching "source" (and "symbol filter" if set) — this is fan-out, not disambiguation. Comma-separate multiple destinations/symbols.

Providers & analysts
